Source settings

Saved on this server. Changes to sources apply to new streams.

The total time allowed to prepare a selected stream for playback, including source inspection. Listing never reads source media; prepare or play any stream to try it.

Audio alignment

Length of the short English clip taken from the upstream video. DubLift looks for that clip in a longer Vixsrc audio section. A longer clip can improve matching but needs more upstream video data. Default: 5 seconds.

How far earlier or later the matching Vixsrc audio may be. With a 5-second sample and radius 10, DubLift searches about 25 seconds of Vixsrc audio for a 5-second upstream clip. A larger radius uses more Vixsrc audio; it does not lengthen the upstream clip. With immediate MKV/MP4 playback, DubLift waits until the player has downloaded the part containing that clip, about 15 seconds from the chosen playback position by default.

How many places in the movie DubLift checks. One finishes sooner. Two or more also check later in the movie, helping catch audio that goes out of sync. DubLift needs at least two matching checks before it can send Italian audio to the player unchanged.
